# 🌈 Personas: the helpers of PIR®'s Discord

Every bot and agent that speaks in PIR®'s spaces has a name, a voice and a clear job, so members always know
**who** is talking and **why**. The names honor our ethos: recovery, set and setting, and the sacred medicines,
sacraments and catalysts our fellowship talks about, always with reverence for the lineages they come from and
never as encouragement to use anything. *Proposed by Alfred for Christopher and TechCom; final names are theirs.*

## ☄️ The agent fleet: **C.A.T.A.L.Y.S.T.**

> 🎨 **Meet them on the chalkboard:** https://psychedelicsinrecovery.github.io/catalyst-desk-public/ (the fleet's
> showcase, with the hand-built emblem, the art and how the helpers work together).

**C.A.T.A.L.Y.S.T. — *Collaborative Agents Tending And Lifting Your Service Together.*** Named for PIR®'s own
category of *Medicines, Sacraments & Catalysts*. A catalyst helps a change happen without being consumed by it:
it doesn't do the healing, it helps the conditions for healing. That's how we want behind-the-scenes help to feel.

C.A.T.A.L.Y.S.T. is **one Discord app** (the "ops face") that posts announcements, keeps channel descriptions
tidy and can tend private channels, **without** reading members' messages. Each agent signs its posts and carries
a medicine as its emblem:

| Part of the tree | Mycelial name | Stands for | What it is |

Proving you belong means tracing your own path from your spore, along the rhizomorphs, to the root, without ever
showing anyone else's.
