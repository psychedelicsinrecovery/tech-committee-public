is the boilerplate default, not custom-written content. Distinct from the main site's Privacy
Policy (`psychedelicsinrecovery-org/safety-and-ethics/privacy-policy.md`), which is its own,
separately maintained page.

Suggested text: Our website address is: http://service.psychedelicsinrecovery.org.

### Comments
Suggested text: When visitors leave comments on the site we collect the data shown in the comments
form, and also the visitor's IP address and browser user agent string to help spam detection. An
anonymized hash of your email may be checked against Gravatar; your profile picture becomes public
alongside an approved comment.

### Media
Suggested text: avoid uploading images with embedded location data (EXIF GPS) — visitors can
extract it.

### Cookies
Suggested text: comment cookies last one year; login cookies last two days (two weeks with
"Remember Me"); screen-option cookies last a year; an edit-cookie (no personal data) expires after
1 day.

### Embedded content from other websites
Suggested text: embedded content behaves as if the visitor visited the other site directly, and may
track interaction the same way.

### Who we share your data with
Suggested text: if you request a password reset, your IP address is included in the reset email.

Suggested text: comments and metadata are retained indefinitely to auto-approve follow-ups;

### What rights you have over your data
Suggested text: registered users/commenters can request an export or erasure of their personal
data, except what's legally required to be retained.

### Where your data is sent
