## Get Involved

**Want to serve on the PR Committee?**

PR service is a meaningful way to give back to PIR's mission and support the sharing of recovery
resources in the world.

**Typical contributions include:**
- Writing and editing copy
- Supporting outreach initiatives
- Editing and managing content to our social media platforms
- Assisting with coordination of PR activities

No prior PR experience required — just a desire to serve and a willingness to support PIR's service
vision with care. Email: [pr@psychedelicsinrecovery.org](mailto:pr@psychedelicsinrecovery.org)

### About Us

The **PR Committee** is a trusted servant group within the PIR® service structure dedicated to
communication, awareness, and public interface. Our role is to support PIR's growth and service by
sharing accurate information about PIR's recovery-focused mission, service opportunities, and
fellowship resources with broader communities — including members, professionals, partners, and the
public.

We are not a marketing team — we are a *service committee*, rooted in the Twelve Step ethos of
honesty, humility, and service. Our work reflects PIR's values: to carry the message, uphold
anonymity, and support recovery journeys.

### Mission Statement

**Our Purpose & Mission** — The PR Committee exists to:
- **Clarify PIR's Purpose:** Explain who we are as a recovery fellowship that integrates 12-Step
  principles and mindful engagement with psychedelics as one potential tool in recovery.
- **Share Information Responsibly:** Ensure that PIR's voice in the world is accurate, respectful,
  and aligned with our recovery purpose — neither promoting substances nor encouraging illegal
  activity.
- **Support Access to Recovery Resources:** Facilitate access to PIR service information, meeting
  resources, educational materials, and opportunities to serve.
- **Connect with Communities & Partners:** Build bridges with individuals, support networks, allies
  in recovery, and recovery-oriented organisations who share a common interest in compassionate
  support and healing, whilst practising
  [tradition 6](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/PIR_12-Traditions_2025.pdf).
- **Promote Awareness of PIR Fellowship:** Increase awareness of PIR's existence, structure, and
  service opportunities while honouring anonymity and avoiding controversy.

**Help PIR® connect with the World — with Clarity, Respect, and Purpose**
