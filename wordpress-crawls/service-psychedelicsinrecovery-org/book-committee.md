## Book Committee

#### Psychedelics in Recovery: Medicines, Sacraments and Catalysts

A project organized to fulfill the mission of editing and publishing PIR's first book. Changed from a "committee" model to a "project" model — a working group open only to volunteers willing to dedicate significant time to editing, writing, and producing the book. The editor's edition was distributed in PDF format October 2025 due to publication delays, so the fellowship could see it sooner. Now in final editing stages — no longer seeking or able to train volunteers.

## Get Involved

Typical contribution: writing and editing copy. No prior book-editing experience required. Email: forapirbook@gmail.com

## About Us

Founded by Clifton Ross at the November 2022 Psychedelics in Recovery Intergroup meeting. Initially organized forums to gather testimony and coordinated volunteers; reorganized as a working group with Cheryl Stevens as co-editor and Kolya as project manager after initial enthusiasm gave way to the hard work of assembly. Other PIR members have volunteered to copy-edit and give feedback. Book is in final production stages.

## Mission Statement

The Book Committee's objective remains the writing, editing, production and publication of "Psychedelics in Recovery: Medicines, Sacraments and Catalysts."
