### Personal recovery depends on fellowship unity

The mission of PIR® is to educate and support the addiction recovery community for individuals to
have a safe space to explore addiction recovery options. The Board of Directors serves as the
steward of the organization's mission, legal responsibilities, and long-term sustainability.
Guided by PIR®'s primary purpose, to carry a message of recovery to anyone who may be seeking or
in need of it, the Board supports the fellowship by providing oversight, continuity, and
strategic direction in response to community need.

## About Us

The Board of Directors is the governance body of Psychedelics in Recovery™ (PIR®), a nonprofit
organization dedicated to supporting individuals in recovery who are interested in mindful,
responsible engagement with psychedelics within a recovery-oriented framework.

The Board does not manage the day-to-day operations of PIR®'s recovery meetings or speak on behalf
of individual members. Instead, it exists to provide structure, continuity, and accountability —
supporting the fellowship while respecting the autonomy of committees and the lived experience of
members.

Board service is rooted in humility, responsibility, and service, reflecting PIR®'s commitment to
ethical leadership and recovery-centered values.

## Our Responsibilities

- **Governance & Oversight:** Ensuring PIR® operates in alignment with its mission, bylaws, and
  nonprofit obligations, including fiduciary, legal, and ethical responsibilities.
- **Strategic Direction:** Providing long-term vision and guidance to support PIR®'s growth,
  sustainability, and evolving service needs.
- **Organizational Support:** Supporting committees, officers, and trusted servants by clarifying
  roles, approving major initiatives, and helping remove structural or administrative barriers.
- **Fiduciary Stewardship:** Overseeing finances, budgeting, compliance, and responsible use of
  Seventh Tradition funds in alignment with PIR®'s values.

## Our Purpose & Mission

The Board of Directors exists to:

- Safeguard PIR®'s mission — ensure organizational decisions remain aligned with PIR®'s primary
  purpose and recovery-centered ethos.
- Provide ethical & legal oversight — fulfill nonprofit governance responsibilities, including
  compliance, financial stewardship, and risk management.
- Support fellowship-driven service — facilitate access to PIR® service information, meeting
  resources, educational materials, and opportunities to serve.
- Ensure organizational continuity — maintain institutional memory, leadership transitions, and
  long-term sustainability.
- Uphold principles before personalities — model collaborative, values-based leadership reflecting
  PIR®'s recovery roots and service traditions.
