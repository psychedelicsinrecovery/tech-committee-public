## ForaPIR

**Purpose:** To update and inform the PIR general body of book projects, personal stories and
potential published material.

**Actions:** Committee reports monthly; membership-wide real-time voting quarterly.

**Membership:** Committee Chairs and ForaPIR members can vote. Can create working groups in situ
to follow up next month or prepare for a quarterly vote.

**Suggested Sobriety/Service Requirements:** Recovery stability as defined and accepted by the
membership committee.
