## PR COMMITTEE

#### Carry the message to other seeking recovery

Public relations within PIR® is guided by our primary purpose of carrying the message of recovery. PIR® seeks to communicate with care, clarity, and humility, placing principles before personalities. Through thoughtful communication, collaboration, and community outreach, we help share PIR's mission, values, and service opportunities with clarity, integrity, and alignment to our guiding principles.

## Our Current Projects

- **De Vine newsletter** — launched January 2026, monthly format following the 12 spiritual principles, updating fellows on committee activity.
- **Festivals and gatherings** — exploring events where PIR could host meetings or recovery-centered spaces. Recommend an event: pr@psychedelicsinrecovery.org
- **PIR Integration Radio** — launching its first series, "Journeys of healing," interviews with PIR members about their experience of psychedelics in recovery.

## Get Involved

**Want to serve on the PR Committee?**

Typical contributions: writing/editing copy, supporting outreach initiatives, editing/managing social media content, assisting with coordination of PR activities. No prior PR experience required. Email: pr@psychedelicsinrecovery.org

## About Us

The PR Committee is a trusted servant group within the PIR® service structure dedicated to communication, awareness, and public interface — not a marketing team, but a service committee rooted in the Twelve Step ethos of honesty, humility, and service.

## Mission Statement

- Clarify PIR's Purpose — explain who we are as a recovery fellowship that integrates 12-Step principles and mindful engagement with psychedelics.
- Share Information Responsibly — accurate, respectful communication aligned with recovery purpose; neither promoting substances nor encouraging illegal activity.
- Support Access to Recovery Resources — meeting resources, educational materials, service opportunities.
- Connect with Communities & Partners — building bridges while practicing Tradition 6.
- Promote Awareness of PIR Fellowship — while honouring anonymity and avoiding controversy.
