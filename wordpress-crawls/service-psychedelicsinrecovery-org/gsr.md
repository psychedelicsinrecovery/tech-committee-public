## Group Service Representatives

**GSR Committee:** To introduce GSRs to General Service Traditions (Concepts), educate GSRs on
service structure, help GSRs with meeting business materials or procedures.

**Purpose:** To facilitate the flow of information from Intergroup to Meetings.

**Actions:** Meet monthly to receive reports from Intergroup, and give reports from meetings.

**Scope of Service:** Help new members find resources/info, make reports, help run business
meetings.

**Membership:** One GSR per meeting.

**Sobriety/Service Requirements:** 6 months self-defined clean time, 3 months minimum meeting-
level service or equivalent to Area Committee.
