## GSR COMMITTEE

#### Bridging groups and the Fellowship

The role of a Group Service Representative is one of quiet spiritual responsibility. GSRs serve as living bridges — carrying the voice, needs, and conscience of individual meetings into the wider fellowship, and returning clarity, guidance, and shared wisdom back to the groups they represent. Rooted in humility rather than authority.

## What is the GSR committee?

A trusted servant group — a reunion of all PIR General Service Representatives, coming together to serve the fellowship as a whole. Information flows both ways between individual meetings and PIR service.

At meetings, representatives:
- Receive updates and news about what's happening across the PIR fellowship
- Bring forward concerns, questions, or needs from their home groups
- Seek guidance and work collaboratively to find solutions
- Share experience, strength, and hope through service

New ideas are born here — GSRs can propose new initiatives, committees, or changes. Through group conscience, members discuss proposals, bring them back to groups, and vote on matters affecting PIR as a whole.

## When do we meet?

Third Saturday of the month at 9am PST — link on the [Service calendar](https://service.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/service-calendar/).
