# PIR Tech Committee

The Tech Committee meets regularly to manage the fellowship website, Zoom meetings, weekly digest mailer, determine best digital practices, and support the fellowship in online spaces.

Email the tech committee for changes in meetings, website listings, Zoom issues, or anything tech related: tech@psychedelicsinrecovery.org

**Membership:** Open to all with a desire to support PIR in the online universe.
**Sobriety/Service requirements:** 3 months self-defined recovery.
