## Tech Committee

### A Sacred Responsibility in a Digital Fellowship

The Tech Committee serves at one of the most sensitive intersections within Psychedelics in
Recovery: the meeting point between human vulnerability, altered states, and digital systems. For
many, technology is not peripheral — it is the front door: a search query, a shared link, a Zoom
room, a late-night scroll that turns into a moment of hope. Those who serve in tech often never
meet the people they help, yet their work may be the first point of contact for someone
struggling, searching, or quietly reaching out. This is stewardship, not just technical work.

In a fellowship that is largely online, technology is the connective tissue — Zoom rooms, emails,
data systems that protect anonymity and trust, blogs, newsletters, and the flows/systems that make
participation easier. When it's working well, it's invisible; when it fails, the human cost can be
real. Tech Committee members are connected everywhere — not as gatekeepers, but as servants
ensuring continuity, safety, and ease.

## Getting Involved

The Tech Committee does not require technical mastery, professional credentials, or advanced
expertise. What it requires:

- A willingness to build tools most people will never notice
- Care for clarity, accessibility, and simplicity
- Patience with systems and people
- Respect for anonymity and data ethics
- A service-oriented mindset

Some members bring design, development, writing, or social-media experience; others bring
curiosity, reliability, or a willingness to learn. This work is not about being seen — it's about
making things easier for others.

## About Us

Technology in a psychedelic recovery space is never purely technical — it is part art (how
something feels, reads, flows, lands), part science (systems, data, reliability, precision), and
always human (shaped by vulnerability, trust, and care). The committee operates at this
intersection between ancient human experiences and modern digital containers, asking: does this
make it easier for someone to find support without being overwhelmed, exposed, or directed? Not a
marketing team — a service committee rooted in the Twelve Step ethos of honesty, humility, and
service.

## Our Purpose & Mission

- **Steward PIR's digital spaces:** maintain and care for websites, meeting platforms,
  communication systems, and data so they remain stable, secure, accessible, and trustworthy.
- **Support access to recovery:** ensure people searching for recovery resources can reliably find
  accurate information, meeting access, service opportunities, and educational materials.
- **Practice neutrality & restraint:** hold technology with humility and responsibility, remaining
  neutral in messaging and design, avoiding curating/persuading/influencing personal beliefs.
- **Protect trust, anonymity & safety:** safeguard personal data, privacy, and anonymity across all
  digital platforms.
- **Create invisible tools in service of connection:** develop tools, systems, and workflows that
  reduce friction and simplify participation, often unseen.
- **Bridge technology, humanity & recovery:** serve at the intersection of human connection,
  psychedelic recovery, and modern technology, recognizing real-world impact for individuals
  seeking help.
