## PIR Committees

## Committees in Service to the Fellowship

Committees are where the fellowship transforms shared values into sustained actions — trusted servant groups formed to carry specific responsibilities on behalf of Psychedelics in Recovery® as a whole. Rather than centralizing authority, committees distribute service, allowing members to contribute skills, experience, and care in focused, collaborative ways.

Each committee supports the health, unity, and continuity of the fellowship — structure without hierarchy, guidance without control, continuity without rigidity.

# Our Current Committees

**General Service Representative (GSR) Committee** — primary link between individual meetings and the broader PIR® service structure; supports GSRs in service principles, communication, and group conscience.

**Public Relations (PR) Committee** — responsible for how PIR® is presented to the world; outreach, public-facing communication, informational efforts, honoring anonymity, integrity, humility.

**Technology (Tech) Committee** — supports the digital infrastructure of the fellowship: websites, online platforms, technical tools.

**Literature Committee** — oversees creation, review, maintenance of PIR® written materials beyond the primary books; pamphlets, guides, resources.

**Book Committee — Medicines, Sacraments, and Catalysts** — development and stewardship of the book emerging from the fellowship's collective experience; coordinating contributors, organizing testimony, guiding editorial decisions.

**Convention Committee** — focused on the PIR convention (see `convention-committee.md`).
