## Service Structure Subcommittee

**Purpose:** To develop a Service Structure for PIR.

**Actions:** This working group meets monthly for discussions, explorations, info-sharing and
brainstorming. Drawing from individual members' experience in service, the group is visioning,
shaping and developing a Service Structure to serve PIR now and into the future.

**Scope of Service:** Attend the monthly meetings held on last Saturdays at 10:00 Pacific /
1:00 Eastern. Participate in discussions by listening and learning from each other, sharing
service experience at whatever level. No prior service experience required — bring an open mind
and fresh ideas and contribute to the group's conscience. Acquire and apply a working knowledge
of service to help develop a structure for PIR.

**Membership:** Open to all for feedback, input.

**Sobriety/Service Requirements:** None at this time.
