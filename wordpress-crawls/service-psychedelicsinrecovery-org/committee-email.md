## Committee Email Accounts — Guidelines, Setup, and Support

As a committee head or trusted servant, you are provided with a `@psychedelicsinrecovery.org`
email address. This account exists to support service work, communication, and continuity within
the fellowship. These email accounts are role-based, not personal, and may be passed to future
trusted servants when service positions rotate.

## Representing a Service Position

When using your PIR email account:

- You are communicating as a service position, not as a private individual.
- Email content should remain respectful, clear, and fellowship-focused.
- Avoid using the account for personal correspondence, outside business/promotions, or non-PIR
  organizations/unrelated causes.
- Assume emails may be forwarded, referenced later, or reviewed for continuity during service
  transitions.

## How You Can Access Your Email

**Option 1: Webmail (no setup required)** — https://webmail.s33.wpx.net/

**Option 2: Email apps (recommended — IMAP)** — iPhone/iPad, Android, Outlook, Apple Mail,
Thunderbird, or other standard clients.

### Recommended settings (IMAP, secure)

- **Incoming (IMAP):** server `s33.wpx.net`, port `993`, SSL/TLS, username = full email address
- **Outgoing (SMTP):** server `s33.wpx.net`, port `465` or `587`, SSL/TLS, authenticated

### Why IMAP instead of POP3

IMAP keeps email synchronized across devices and on the server, so messages remain available for
future committee heads. POP3 downloads to one device and often removes messages from the server,
causing continuity problems — not appropriate for service roles.

## Email FAQs

**Do I have to use this email address?** Yes — it's the official communication channel for the
role, ensuring continuity and clear boundaries between personal and service communication.

**Can I forward to my personal email?** Not recommended — can break message continuity and create
security issues. Contact the Tech Committee first if forwarding feels necessary.

**Can I change the password?** Not directly — notify the Tech Committee. Passwords are reset for
security, during role transitions, or if compromise is suspected.

**What happens when my service term ends?** The account stays with the position; password is
reset and access transitioned to the next trusted servant.

**Why not just use personal email?** Keeps boundaries clear, prevents confusion during
transitions, preserves institutional memory, protects personal privacy.

**Is my email secure?** Yes, when configured with SSL/TLS encryption, secure ports (993/465/587),
and authenticated sending.

**What if my account is compromised?** Contact the Tech Committee immediately.

**Can I use multiple devices?** Yes — that's a benefit of IMAP; all devices stay in sync.

**Not tech savvy?** Use webmail only, or ask the Tech Committee for step-by-step help.

**Who do I contact for help?** The Tech Committee, for setup, passwords, access, or security
questions.
