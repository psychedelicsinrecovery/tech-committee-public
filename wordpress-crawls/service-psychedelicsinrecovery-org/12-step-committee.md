#### Carry the message to other seeking recovery

PIR®'s 12-Step Committee is finalizing a book that unpacks PiR®'s 12 recovery Steps. PiR® is diverse: people who suffer from addiction, compulsion, codependency, over-functioning, rescuing — different outsides, similar insides. Writing these steps for anyone tired of cycles that cost integrity, peace, or a sense of self.

## Get Involved

**Want to help in the final edits?** Whether you come with 12-Step experience or not, feedback on the drafts is welcome. Email: twelvesteps@psychedelicsinrecovery.org

## About Us

A trusted servant committee within the PIR® service structure dedicated to writing a recovery program specifically for the diverse fellowship of PIR®. Focused on understanding PIR®'s various backgrounds of addictive or co-addictive patterns — listening carefully, writing clearly, returning to the basics (humility, honesty, willingness, amends, prayer/meditation, service) while making the path more usable for people who want an alternative to traditional steps.

## Mission Statement

Writing a 12-step program for people in the PIR® fellowship who want to address their addictive and other problematic behaviors and who feel more at home in PIR® than in other fellowships for which they may otherwise qualify.
