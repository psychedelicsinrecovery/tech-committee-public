### A Bridge Between Experience and Expression

The mission of the Literature Committee (LitCom) is to carry the message of recovery in written
form by stewarding the clarity, integrity, and unity of Psychedelics in Recovery™ literature, so
that the heart of the 12 Steps and 12 Traditions may be expressed faithfully, inclusively, and
responsibly for all who seek recovery. Guided by group conscience and loving service, LitCom
attends to the written voice of PIR®, trusting that through humility, collaboration, and care, the
message of recovery may continue to be shared clearly with those within the fellowship and those
still seeking.

## The Written Voice of PIR

LitCom serves as steward of PIR®'s written voice, ensuring all published materials reflect PIR®'s
Steps, Traditions, Guiding Principles, and shared commitment to safety, inclusivity, unity, and
spiritual integrity. Working with the Public Relations and Tech Committees, LitCom reviews, edits,
and finalizes written content — newsletter articles, blog posts, scripts, educational materials,
and member-facing resources — so language remains clear, accessible, compassionate, and aligned
with PIR®'s primary purpose.

LitCom's role is not to govern, promote outside issues, or endorse individual opinions, but to
help preserve coherence, humility, and consistency across PIR® literature while honoring both
lived experience and group conscience.

## Get Involved

Literature service is a meaningful way to give back to PIR's mission and support the sharing of
recovery resources. Typical contributions: writing and editing copy, editing and managing content.
No prior literature experience required. Email: litcomm@psychedelicsinrecovery.org

## Guiding Insight for Our Work

The committee is guided by words from A.A. co-founder Bill W. describing the influence of recovery
literature as "incalculable" — from a 1964 A.A. Grapevine piece on how, without standard
literature, A.A.'s message would have become "hopelessly garbled" and the fellowship would have
"bogged down in a welter of controversy and disunity." This grounds LitCom's service in humility,
responsibility, and unity. Not a marketing team — a service committee rooted in the Twelve Step
ethos of honesty, humility, and service.

## Literature Committee Responsibilities

- Review, editing, and refinement of written material for clarity, consistency, tone, accuracy,
  and alignment with PIR®'s Steps, Traditions, Guiding Principles, and Safety Guidelines.
- Alignment with PIR® core literature — Preamble, Steps, Traditions, Integration language, FAQs,
  and other conference-approved texts.
- Compassionate and inclusive language stewardship, respectful of the diversity of recovery paths
  in the fellowship.
- Finalization and readiness for publication, coordinating with the Public Relations Committee.
- Consistency across platforms — newsletters, blogs, scripts, websites, and other official PIR®
  member materials.
- Inter-committee collaboration (Public Relations, Tech).
- Honoring PIR® unity and primary purpose; attending to language that could unintentionally drift
  into endorsement, outside issues, or misrepresentation of PIR® as a fellowship.
