# Website Re-Launch

*August 17, 2024 — by d lee*

### Welcome to the New Psychedelics in Recovery Website: A Cutting-Edge Space for Inclusive, Forward-Focused Recovery

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our newly remodeled Psychedelics in Recovery website! This isn't just a redesign; it's a reimagining of what a recovery space can be — dynamic, inclusive, and focused on the evolving future of recovery.

#### Pioneering the Future of Recovery

Our remodeled website reflects the philosophy that the journey of healing and growth is a path best traveled together, bringing to life a vibrant, interactive space where members can connect, share, and support one another. Our approach integrates the powerful potential of psychedelics as tools for healing within a safe, supportive, and diverse environment.

#### A Dynamic, Forward-Focused Space

Recovery is not a static process; it's a journey that evolves with us. The site is designed as an interactive, living space that adapts and grows alongside the community, with regularly updated content, new features, and opportunities for engagement.

#### Inclusivity at Our Core

Inclusivity is at the heart of the mission — a welcoming space for people of all backgrounds, beliefs, and experiences, with expanded resources and support systems so every voice is heard.

#### What's New?

- **Enhanced User Experience** — easier navigation, fresh modern design.
- **Expanded Resources** — guides, tools, insights, research, and personal stories of growth and healing.
- **Community-Focused Features** — discussions, peer connection, events.
- **Mobile Friendly** — stay connected on the go.

#### Join Us on This Journey

Welcome to the new Psychedelics in Recovery, where the future of recovery begins today.
