## Embracing Inclusivity and Diversity Beyond Traditional Boundaries

This group recognizes that recovery is not a linear process and that each person's journey is unique. We welcome individuals from all backgrounds, including those of different races, ethnicities, gender identities, sexual orientations, and religious beliefs.

**Psychedelics in Recovery™** is a groundbreaking support group that reimagines the landscape of addiction recovery by integrating the transformative potential of psychedelics within a framework of radical inclusivity and diversity, going beyond the limitations of traditional abstinence-based recovery programs.

### A New Paradigm of Recovery

Traditional recovery programs have often been shaped by a one-size-fits-all approach focused on abstinence and uniformity. While invaluable to many, they have sometimes struggled to fully embrace the diverse experiences and needs of all individuals, particularly marginalized communities. Psychedelics in Recovery offers a flexible, inclusive model that honors each person's individuality and cultural background.

### Inclusivity as a Core Value

We welcome individuals from all backgrounds — races, ethnicities, gender identities, sexual orientations, religious beliefs. Meetings are structured to foster open dialogue and mutual respect, ensuring everyone feels safe to share without fear of judgment or exclusion.

### Honoring Cultural and Spiritual Diversity

Unlike programs that adopt a uniform spiritual framework, PIR honors and celebrates diverse spiritual practices and cultural traditions members bring — indigenous wisdom, Eastern philosophies, or other spiritual paths — while acknowledging and respecting the historical significance of psychedelics across cultures.

### Beyond Abstinence: A Holistic Approach

For some individuals, psychedelics play a crucial role in healing, offering insights, emotional breakthroughs, and spiritual growth. This holistic approach also extends to complementary practices — mindfulness, meditation, yoga, somatic therapies — supporting integration and long-term recovery.

### Our Commitment to Empowerment

**Inclusivity** means actively welcoming and embracing every individual regardless of background, identity, or experience — every voice acknowledged and celebrated.

**Safety** is the cornerstone: a respectful, non-judgmental environment where people can be vulnerable and authentic without fear of discrimination or harm.

**Diversity** enriches the community — different backgrounds, identities, experiences, and viewpoints, learned from and grown through together.
