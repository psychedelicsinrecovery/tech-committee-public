## Libraries

As members of Psychedelics in Recovery, we are often encouraged to explore, learn, ponder, discover and seek.

Found here are several hundred relevant and obscure digital papers, documents, theses, and books relating to AA history, psychedelics and addictions and recovery. Broken into 2 libraries: Historical Psychedelic and Historical 12-step related.

### Historical Psychedelic and Addiction Research, Papers, Studies and Books

Rarely seen documents including the Towns Hospital Belladonna Cure protocols, various 1950s studies showing tremendous outcomes in the usage of LSD and alcoholism, questionnaires used in research studies, theses and other historically relevant documents and books.

### Historical AA, Oxford Group, Washingtonians, and Miscellaneous

Rarely-digitized materials covering major influences in the formation of Alcoholics Anonymous, including Oxford Group books/documents, New Thought movements, Emmanuel Movement, Keeley Cure, and the 10 original recommended books by early AAs.

*Note: Early AA was heavily influenced by Christian books and themes; these documents are provided for purely historical reasons.*
