## Help us celebrate 10 years!

**PIR® has grown into a global fellowship rooted in connection, service, and recovery. This convention offers something deeper than a meeting: a space to come together intentionally, to reflect on how far we've come, and to shape where we're going.**

**What attendees will experience:**

- **Advance Your Recovery!** Recovery-centric programming: topic workshops, main speaker meetings, community-building events, breakout discussion panels.
- **Share Experience!** Volunteer to lead a workshop or assemble a panel (see signup on registration form).
- **Build a Community!** Meet Zoom-box people in person.
- **See Colorado and Legal Psychedelic Therapy!** Guided outdoor activities, arts/culture experiences, visits to facilities in the local natural medicine industry. Arrive Thursday Oct. 8 for Friday the 9th's activities.
- **Live-Streaming!** For those unable to attend in person. Estimated in-person attendance: 200 people, plus 500+ expected online worldwide.
- Opportunity to connect with committee leaders and volunteers shaping PIR's future.

**Who this event is for**: people in recovery exploring psychedelics in healing; long-time PIR® fellows and newer members; trusted servants shaping the next decade; people curious about the intersection of psychedelics, spirituality, and recovery.

**Speakers / Facilitators**: Peer-led. The Program Committee is seeking volunteers — sign up on the registration form.

**Schedule Outline (subject to change)**

- **Thursday October 8** — Evening dine-around Denver groups
- **Friday October 9** — Morning/afternoon/evening excursions (free and self-pay mix); opening reception, evening
- **Saturday October 10** — Workshops, panels, meetings 7-5; evening dine-arounds/excursions
- **Sunday October 11** — Workshops, panels, meetings 7-5; Closing Circle 5pm; evening excursions
- **Monday October 12** — Columbus/Indigenous Peoples Day holiday, departures

**What attendees will experience**: recovery-focused workshops and speaker meetings; breakout discussions on integration and spiritual principles; panels bridging lived experience, service, and the evolving field; guided outdoor experiences, cultural outings, Colorado recovery community connections; PIR®'s first in-person annual board gathering and collective visioning.
