## Integrating Psychedelics Into Recovery

Integrating psychedelics into traditional abstinence-based recovery is a complex and sensitive process that requires careful consideration, personal guidance, and a strong foundation in recovery principles.

[Read Our Guiding Principles](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/PIR_Guiding-Principles_2026.pdf)

Ten suggestions for someone in traditional abstinence-based recovery who is considering integrating psychedelics into their healing journey:

1. **Consult with a Professional** — Speak with a therapist, counselor, or healthcare professional who has experience with both addiction recovery and psychedelic therapy.
2. **Research Thoroughly** — Educate yourself on the potential risks and benefits of psychedelics, especially in the context of addiction recovery.
3. **Consider the Intentions** — Clarify your intentions for integrating psychedelics. Clear intentions can guide the experience and set boundaries.
4. **Seek a Safe and Supportive Environment** — Ensure you have a trusted guide, therapist, or experienced facilitator.
5. **Integrate Slowly** — Allow ample time between sessions to integrate insights and experiences into your recovery journey.
6. **Join a Community or Support Group** — Connect with others who have experience integrating psychedelics into their recovery.
7. **Stay Connected to Your Recovery Program** — Maintain your connection to 12-step meetings, therapy, or other forms of support.
8. **Monitor for Triggers and Challenges** — Be vigilant about how psychedelic experiences affect your cravings, triggers, and overall mental health.

It's important to note that while some people find healing and growth through the use of psychedelics, others may find that it disrupts their recovery. Proceeding with caution, professional guidance, and a strong support system is essential.
