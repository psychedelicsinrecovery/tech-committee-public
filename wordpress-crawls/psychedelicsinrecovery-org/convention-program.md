# One Month From the Inaugural PIR Convention in Denver!

*September 10, 2026*

We are officially one month out from the [Psychedelics in Recovery Convention](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/convention-2026/)!

After months of planning, the gathering we have been imagining is taking its final shape. This year's convention will bring members of the PIR fellowship together for several days of recovery, connection, learning, reflection, and the chance to spend time with one another in person.

The program reflects the breadth of conversations long taking place throughout our fellowship. Psychedelics may be part of what brought many of us to PIR, but recovery remains at the center of why we come together. Throughout the weekend, we will be creating space to explore the Twelve Steps, the opportunities and challenges presented by psychedelic experiences, and the many ways people find healing, belonging, and sustained recovery.

Programming will include a *Steps Workshop*, giving participants an opportunity to engage more deeply with the Twelve Steps and consider how they continue to inform our individual and collective recovery. The workshop will coincide with the publication of *The Twelve Steps for Psychedelics in Recovery*, a major undertaking by PIR's Book Committee that brings the fellowship's collective experience and evolving understanding of Twelve Step recovery into a resource of its own.

We will also explore the relational and somatic dimensions of this work through *Embodiment: How to Meet Ourselves & Others*, a presentation and discussion focused on what it means to show up more fully in relationship with ourselves and the people around us. An *Art as Preparation & Integration Workshop* will offer another approach, inviting participants to consider creative practice as a way of preparing for, processing, and making meaning from significant experiences.

Several sessions will take on larger questions facing both PIR and the psychedelic community. *Indigenous Perspectives on Recovery & Community* will create space for a panel and group discussion about traditions, community, and different understandings of healing and recovery. *What Can Skillful Integration Achieve?* will examine the possibilities—and limits—of integration when translating psychedelic experiences into meaningful change.

We also believe it is important to make room for the more difficult conversations.

*The Shadow Side of Psychedelics* will bring participants together for a panel and group discussion about the harms, complexities, blind spots, and unintended consequences that can accompany psychedelic use and psychedelic culture. *Neurodiversity + Intersecting Identities* will explore how different identities, lived experiences, and ways of processing the world can shape experiences of recovery and belonging.

A screening of *Psychedelics and Addiction Recovery*, followed by group discussion, will bring these questions to the screen through the lived realities of addiction, psychedelic experience, and the search for lasting recovery. The convention will also intersect with a growing media conversation around these same issues — journalist Sophie Haigney (Ferriss-UC Berkeley Psychedelic Journalism Fellowship) is reporting a magazine story on psychedelics in recovery and will be attending Saturday, interested in hearing personal stories (anonymity preserved). Contact: Sophie.haigney@gmail.com.

And throughout the convention, there will be something even more fundamental to PIR: PIR meetings themselves.

**Beyond Panels and Presentations**

The convention will also include yoga and breathwork, and a *Silent Disco*. Optional off-site activities include small-group day trips, tours of healing centers and grow facilities, wellness/contemplative/community-oriented spaces, shared meals, dine-arounds, informal café gatherings, ecstatic dance, museum and art experiences, and immersive visual experiences.

For an organization that has grown across geographic distance, online meetings, message threads, service committees, and virtual fellowship, there is something uniquely valuable about simply being in the same place. Some of the most meaningful moments of a convention cannot be scheduled: the conversation after a meeting, the people who decide to get lunch together, the walk between events, the person you finally meet after knowing their voice from a screen.

We are looking forward to exploring these questions together and seeing the PIR community gathered in one place. One month to go!
