## Carrying The Message To Others Seeking Recovery

PiR® is a diverse group of people who suffer from addiction, compulsion, codependency, over-functioning, rescuing —different outsides, similar insides— with a desire to address their addictive and other problematic behaviors while feeling more at home than in other fellowships for which they may otherwise qualify

These steps were written for anyone tired of cycles that cost integrity, peace, or a sense of self

[What Was the 12-Step committee?](https://service.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/12-step-committee/)

The **12-Step Committee** consisted of a group of members and trusted servants in the PIR® fellowship who worked diligently to understand specifically the diverse fellowship of PIR® and its various backgrounds of addictive and/or co-addictive patterns

The job was to listen carefully, write clearly, and keep returning to the basics—humility, honesty, willingness, amends, prayer/meditation, and service—while making the path more usable for people who want an alternative to traditional steps.
