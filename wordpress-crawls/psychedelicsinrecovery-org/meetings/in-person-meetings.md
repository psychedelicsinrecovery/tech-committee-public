## In-Person Meetings

Looking for [online meetings](https://www.psychedelicsinrecovery.org/meetings/online-meetings/)?

Our in-person meetings provide an opportunity for members to connect, share, and support one
another. Guided by the principles of mutual aid, these gatherings foster a compassionate
environment where personal insights can be discussed openly, helping each participant strengthen
their recovery journey. Whether you're new to psychedelics or have been working with them for
years, PIR's community-based meetings offer a space of trust, growth, and connection.
